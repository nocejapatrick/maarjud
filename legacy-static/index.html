How we create scrunchies

By hand, a few at a time. Here is what happens before one reaches you.

  1. 1

    Pick the fabric

    Satin, velvet or cotton, chosen for how it catches the light and how it feels in your hand.

  2. 2

    Cut it generous

    Each strip is cut long and wide, so the gather comes out full.

  3. 3

    Sew and thread

    We stitch the strip into a tube, turn it out and run a soft elastic through.

  4. 4

    Finish by hand

    The last seam is closed by hand, and every scrunchie is checked before it is packed.
